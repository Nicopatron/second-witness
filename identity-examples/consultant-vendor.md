# identity-example — the consultant who has to put a recommendation in writing

One of three worked buyer profiles. A real shape of person this researcher serves — and the one closest to why the folder was built.

---

## Who

**Marco, independent AI consultant.** Gets paid to tell a client whether a vendor's pitch survives contact with production. His name goes on the memo. If he's wrong, the client eats a migration and he eats his reputation. He's evaluated enough tools to be skeptical and enough vendors to know the pitches rhyme.

## The decision

Recommend (or kill) a managed AI vendor the client is about to sign a 12-month contract with. The vendor claims SOC 2, "enterprise-grade reliability," and a benchmark win. Marco's deliverable is a written memo with a confidence level he has to defend in a room.

## What's load-bearing for him

- **The actual SOC 2 report** — Type I vs Type II, scope, date — not the badge on the homepage.
- **Real incident history** vs the "99.9%" claim, and whether the SLA payout covers what an outage costs *his client*.
- **The benchmark win's contamination status** — and whether an independent board corroborates it.
- **A defensible confidence level.** He can't write "looks good"; he needs "confirmed on X, biddable on Y, here's the one bet and its cost."

## The paste he'd send

```
Writing a vendor recommendation for a client signing a 12-month contract. Vendor claims SOC 2, 99.9% uptime, and a benchmark win over the incumbent. I need a defensible memo — what holds, what doesn't, and where I should hedge.
```

## What the researcher does differently for him

It behaves most like a peer here, because his question is already well-framed. It tiers each claim, then routes each to its real witness: SOC 2 → request the actual Type II report (verifiable-now, just demand the document); 99.9% → the status-page history via the Wayback Machine + the payout terms, not the marketing (verifiable-now); benchmark win → contamination check first, then a contamination-resistant board (the gap *is* the finding). It hands Marco exactly what a memo needs — a bucketed map with a defensible confidence per claim and the one bet named with its cost — so the structure of the researcher's output *is* the structure of his deliverable.

*Why this profile exists: the buyer whose output format matches the researcher's native output. The two-witness map is already a consulting memo. This is the case the whole folder was modeled on.*
